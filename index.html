Painter｜Stardust Painting

色の宇宙と、詩の星座。
身体の感覚から、世界のひらきを描く。

時任梨乃は、ペインターとして、ペインティング、コラージュ、詩領域を交差・統合しながら、 絵画作品の可能性を拡張させる実験的な制作を行っている。

Painter Rino Tokito explores the expansion of painting by intersecting painting, collage, and poetic fragments.

Through her original technique “Stardust Painting,” she embraces chance as a cosmic phenomenon, treating paint as an event that opens space. Fragments of poetry appear like constellations, while collage—rooted in bodily sensation and memory—invites a sensory opening of the world.
